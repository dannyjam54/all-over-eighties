Meet All Over 80's

The Band

Big melodies. Bigger choruses. The songs that filled dancefloors, soundtracked the best nights and still get everyone singing.

The 80s sound.
Played live.

All Over 80's brings the sound and attitude of an unforgettable decade to the stage. From synth-driven classics and new romantic favourites to massive pop and rock anthems, the band is all about recreating the excitement of the era.

Expect familiar intros, unforgettable hooks and the kind of songs that bring the whole room together.
